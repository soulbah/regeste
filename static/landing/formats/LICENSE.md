# File-format icons

pdf.svg, word.svg, markdown.svg, document.svg come from the Material Icon Theme
(https://github.com/material-extensions/vscode-material-icon-theme), MIT
licensed. They are original Material-style drawings, not the vendors' marks —
Adobe's and Microsoft's logos stay under their own copyright and are not used
here. document.svg is retinted from #42a5f5 to #8a8175 so plain text reads
apart from Markdown; the MIT licence permits modification.
