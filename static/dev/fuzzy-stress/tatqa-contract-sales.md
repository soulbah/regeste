# Sales by contract type

Source fixture: TAT-QA development example `3ffd9053-a45d-491c-957a-1b2fa0af0570` (CC BY 4.0). Values are in millions of dollars.

Substantially all contracts are fixed-price contracts. Other contract types are cost-plus and time-and-material contracts.

| Contract type | 2019 | 2018 | 2017 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| Other | 44.1 | 56.7 | 70.8 |
| Total sales | $1,496.5 | $1,202.9 | $1,107.7 |

On a cost-plus contract, the company receives allowable incurred costs plus a profit. The profit can be fixed or variable, up to funding levels set by the customer.

On a time-and-material contract, the company is paid for direct labor hours at specified fixed hourly rates, plus materials at cost.
